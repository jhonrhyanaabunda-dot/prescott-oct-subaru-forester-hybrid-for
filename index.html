01Why the hybrid fits Prescott

Prescott sits above 5,000 feet. That is where a hybrid Forester earns its keep.

Prescott sits above 5,000 feet, with grade changes, cold mornings, and long highway runs to the Valley. That driving profile is where a hybrid Forester earns its keep. The electric motor helps off the line on a grade, and the system recaptures energy on the long descents down to Phoenix.

The Forester Hybrid pairs Subaru's Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive with an electrified powertrain, which suits the mountain-desert driving most of our Prescott shoppers actually do. Here is what the hybrid keeps from every Forester: Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive stays standard. The raised ground clearance stays. The wide cargo opening and the tall greenhouse stay. The hybrid layer sits on top of a vehicle that already fits Prescott life.

The contrarian part

Long graded descents are exactly where regenerative braking does real work.

Most buyers assume a hybrid only pays off in stop-and-go city traffic, so they write it off for rural Prescott driving. Counterintuitively, the long graded descents around here are exactly where regenerative braking earns its keep, recapturing energy on the way down that a gas-only Forester throws away as heat.

Highway-heavy and mountain driving is not a reason to skip the hybrid. It can be a reason to look harder.

Frequently asked questions.

Findlay Subaru Prescott stocks new Forester models, and the hybrid powertrain is part of the lineup. Does the Forester Hybrid keep all-wheel drive?

Yes. Subaru Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive is standard on the Forester Hybrid, the same as on the gas Forester. The hybrid system works alongside the AWD rather than replacing it. For the full drivetrain breakdown, see the official details at subaru.com.

What is the warranty on a new Forester Hybrid?

New Subaru coverage runs bumper-to-bumper for 3 years or 36,000 miles and powertrain for 5 years or 60,000 miles. Rust perforation coverage runs 5 years with unlimited mileage. Subaru's hybrid system uses a high-voltage battery; ask us to confirm the current battery coverage terms on the specific unit you are considering.

How does the Forester Hybrid compare to the gas Forester?

The hybrid keeps the same cargo room, ground clearance, seating, and AWD as the gas Forester. The difference is the electrified powertrain, which helps efficiency and adds electric assist on grades.
